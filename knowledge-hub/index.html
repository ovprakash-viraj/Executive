CASE STUDIES

Lessons from complex enterprise delivery.

All examples are intentionally anonymized and focus on the delivery problem, decisions and transferable lessons.

MULTI-BUSINESS-UNIT ERP TRANSFORMATION

Phased rollout across a complex enterprise landscape

Challenge: Multiple business units, dependent migrations, shared governance and different readiness levels.

Approach: Wave-based rollout governance, readiness gates, reconciliation controls and focused hypercare.

Lesson: A common platform still needs business-unit-specific readiness evidence.

PROJECT OPERATIONS · DATA & FINANCE

Protecting project margin through reconciliation

Challenge: Project cost completeness was difficult to prove across operational and finance processes.

Approach: Traceability from source transaction through project Actuals, billing and financial outcomes.

Lesson: Reconciliation must validate the financial story, not only record counts.

DELIVERY GOVERNANCE · CHANGE CONTROL

When “small changes” create large delivery impact

Challenge: Individually small requests accumulated schedule, testing and dependency impact.

Approach: Decision criteria covering value, effort, regression, dependency and commercial consequence.

Lesson: Change size should be judged by total delivery impact, not development hours alone.
