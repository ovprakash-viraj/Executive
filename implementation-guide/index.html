ABOUT THE PLAYBOOK

From business intent to sustainable improvement

Enterprise implementation is a business transformation program enabled by technology. This complete guide follows the delivery journey from business case and presales through discovery, solution design, build, data, testing, organizational change, go-live, hypercare and enterprise rollout.

The principles apply broadly to ERP and CRM programs, with Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Power Platform as practical examples. Each chapter combines delivery practices, enterprise examples, implementation checklists and leadership takeaways.
